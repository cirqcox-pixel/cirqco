Medveta

Medveta HMS is one platform for patients, doctors and hospital staff — appointments, records, billing and day-to-day operations, built for multi-facility care across East Africa. Medveta Distribution is the sibling product for pharmaceutical wholesalers, already in production.

The challenge

A health system built to be better, not just present.

Medveta HMS is Cirqco's biggest bet in healthcare: a hospital management system built to a standard we believe can meet or exceed what patients and providers get from many existing health systems today. We say that as a challenge we're setting for ourselves, not a claim we've already proven — and we'd rather be judged against that bar than a lower one.

That ambition only means something if it's built alongside the institutions already responsible for health outcomes — ministries, hospital networks and health-focused NGOs working on the same problem are the first people we want at the table.

Medveta Distribution

A sibling product to HMS — not mixed into the hospital stack. Wholesalers still run stock in an ERP, chase pharmacy orders on WhatsApp, and discover expiry when it is already late. Medveta Distribution is the B2B layer on top of systems they already use — not an ERP replacement.

Each customer gets an independent deployment and PostgreSQL database. Cirqco Control sells packages and licences; the instance consumes them. Retail pharmacies order as external customers — they are never staff.

  • StockCatalogue, batches, FEFO, receiving and branch ledgers.
  • B2BInvite pharmacies; preferred branch; pickup or delivery preference.
  • DataCSV onboarding for products, stock and customers while live ERP sync is pending.
