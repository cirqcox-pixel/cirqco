Intelligence pipeline

Detect. Analyze. Identify. Alert.

Sentinel processes the physical environment at the edge — on your property — and stores only the intelligence and evidence your policies require. Raw motion does not page anyone. Qualified incidents reach security; management gets briefs, not a firehose of alerts.

  • DetectPerson, vehicle, livestock and perimeter signals from your IP cameras and sensors — hardware-agnostic, standards-first.
  • AnalyzeOn-site AI at Sentinel Edge qualifies observations into incidents and daily activity — theft, access, damage, herd movement, pasture patterns — before anything notifies.
  • IdentifyProgressive identity resolution for people and ear-tag accounting for herd stock. Similarity is never verification; every important action is an auditable event.
  • AlertSecurity officers get qualified incidents on Command and mobile desks. Managers get structured day briefs — how the site behaved, not a firehose of raw motion.

Sentinel Farm · Animal control

Know how your herd behaved — even when you are somewhere else.

Pasture cameras, ear-tag reads and gate coverage turn a farm or ranch into an intelligently monitored property. Sentinel tracks herd stock through the day, surfaces pasture activity in Command, and keeps perimeter and vehicle coverage on the same backbone as your office or estate.

How your animals' day looks in Command

Open the Animals surface from anywhere with connectivity and see what happened on the ground: who was on site, when tracks started and ended, which pasture or gate camera last sighted an ear tag, and how activity broke down across the day — without standing in the field.

  • RegistryHerd accounting for cattle, goats and other stock — ear-tag reads fill the registry as pasture cameras report.
  • TracksLive and historical livestock tracks — count, timing and location by zone and camera, not guesswork at dusk.
  • AnalyticsDay-level activity breakdown: pasture movement, detections and qualified events in one operational timeline.
  • GatesCameras on pens, gates and perimeter lines — security around stock movement, not just people.

Paddock access when plans change

Rotation day and you are delayed on the road? Gate and access workflows let authorised staff release the next paddock from Command or the Gate desk — controlled crossing, auditable event, no one climbing a fence because the herd needs to move.

How a Sentinel site is built

From the camera to command — one connected security environment per property. Where terrestrial internet is inadequate, connectivity comes first: Starlink feeds the security network, Sentinel Edge records and qualifies locally, and Security Command gives operators the operational view.

Property protection continues when the internet is down. When connectivity returns, intelligence synchronizes — events, identity metadata and selected evidence — not continuous video uploads.
